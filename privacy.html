Privacy & Browser Storage

This website keeps data collection intentionally minimal. Below is a plain-language explanation of what is stored in your browser and what is not tracked.

What we store locally

  • Theme preference — whether you selected dark mode or light mode.
  • Announcement dismissal — whether you closed the top announcement bar for the current browser session.
  • Cookie notice dismissal — whether you already acknowledged the site notice.

What we do not do

  • We do not run third-party analytics on this site.
  • We do not place ad-tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or share browsing activity from this website.

Contact forms

If you choose to submit a contact form, the details you provide are sent only for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. If the form is not yet configured, you can always reach us directly via WhatsApp or email.
