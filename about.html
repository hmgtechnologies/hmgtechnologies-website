Our Origin

Where HMG Technologies Came From

HMG Technologies is the technology and data arm of HMG Concepts (His Marvellous Grace Educational Consult), founded by Adewale Samson Adeagbo in 2015. What began as a one-person tutoring service grew into a conviction: that the problems visible every day in Nigerian classrooms and organisations could be solved with technology — if the right person built the right tools.

The name matters. His Marvellous Grace is not branding. It is a genuine conviction that every breakthrough arrived as a result of grace that was not engineered on our own — we just kept showing up and doing the work. That conviction shapes every decision we make.

"We don't build technology for its own sake. We build it because a problem in the classroom, the boardroom, the office, or the community demands a solution." — Adewale Samson Adeagbo, Founder
02
The Method

What AI-Augmented Solutions Development Actually Means

CBT Pro — our flagship product — was built on an Android tablet using Acode editor, with no laptop, no web development background, and zero budget. It is now live in Nigerian classrooms. That is the proof point that defines our working method.

AI-Augmented Solutions Development means using clear thinking and AI leverage to operate competently in any technical domain — building solutions that go beyond what a single discipline, a limited budget, or a constrained environment would traditionally allow. The tool is not the skill. The clarity of thought is the skill. AI is the leverage. Together, they produce what looks like magic — and is actually just disciplined, problem-first thinking applied at scale.

This is not a marketing claim. It is a verifiable working method with 34 live deployed projects across 7 industries to demonstrate it.

Our Values

What We Stand For

🧩
Problem First, Always

We never start with a technology. We start with a problem. If the problem is not clearly understood, no solution will be genuinely useful.

🇳🇬
Built for Nigerian Realities

Slow connections, low-end devices, limited budgets. Everything we build accounts for these realities — not as exceptions, but as the primary design constraint.

📖
Honest Documentation

We document what worked AND what didn't. Data leakage fixes, underperforming models, real metrics. Trust is built on honesty, not curated success stories.

💰
Cost-Effective by Principle

CBT Pro was built with ₦0. That discipline is not an exception — it is how we work. The constraint is the brief, not the barrier.

🤝
Partnership, Not Delivery

We don't hand you a tool and disappear. We train your team, document everything, and remain available. Tools should be understood, not just handed over.

✝️
Faith as Foundation

His Marvellous Grace Educational Consult is named for a genuine conviction. Integrity, authentic service, and community impact are not aspirational — they are operational.
