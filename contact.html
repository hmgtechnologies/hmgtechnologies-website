Frequently Asked Questions
Is CBT Pro really free for schools?▼
Yes. CBT Pro is completely free for schools to use. There is no licence fee, no subscription, and no hidden cost. It was built because Nigerian teachers needed it — and we wanted the barrier to be zero. What you may pay for is custom deployment support or additional features beyond the standard platform. Contact us to discuss your specific needs.
How long does a custom project take?▼
It depends entirely on the scope and complexity. Simple web tools or dashboards: 1–3 weeks. Full ML pipelines: 2–6 weeks. Complex multi-feature platforms: 4–10 weeks. We give you a transparent timeline estimate before any work begins — and we stick to it.
Do you work with small budgets?▼
Yes. We are specifically designed for Nigerian realities — and that includes limited budgets. Our AI-Augmented Development method lets us deliver more than traditional development budgets allow. Tell us what you need and what you can afford. We'll tell you honestly what's achievable within that constraint.
Do you provide training after delivery?▼
Always. We don't hand over tools without making sure your team knows how to use them. All engagements include documentation, handover training, and a post-delivery support window. We want the tools we build to be genuinely used — not abandoned because nobody understood them.
Can I see live examples of your work first?▼
Absolutely. That's exactly what our Portfolio page and Products page are for. Every project has live links you can visit right now. No demos, no mockups — real deployed tools. Go and test them. If what you see matches what you need, let's talk.
